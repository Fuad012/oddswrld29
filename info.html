Help

Everything about ordering from ODDWRLD.

Customer care

Questions about sizing, an order or a restock? Message us on WhatsApp or email email.

Shipping

We deliver across Nigeria. The delivery fee and timing depend on your location and are confirmed with you on WhatsApp before you pay.

Orders & payments

1. Add pieces to your bag and fill in your delivery details.

2. Place your order on WhatsApp (or email). It arrives as a ready-made message.

3. We confirm availability, the delivery fee and payment details with you.

4. Once payment is confirmed, we dispatch your order. No payment is taken on this website.

Returns

If something isn't right, message us within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference and a photo, and we'll make it right.

FAQ

How do the sizes fit?

Most pieces are cut relaxed, as shown in our photos. Pick your usual size, and if you're between sizes message us before ordering.

Can I change my order?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your order reference before payment is confirmed.

Will sold-out pieces come back?

Some will. Join the list on the home page to hear about restocks first.

Privacy

We only use the details you give us to process your order and, if you opt in, to send you updates about new drops.
